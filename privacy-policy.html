Privacy Policy

Wagon Panic · effective October 31, 2026

This policy explains how the Wagon Panic Android game processes information. It applies to the game, not to third-party websites or services that may be linked from it.

1. Data controller and contact

The data controller is Jiri Gerle.

For privacy questions or requests, contact: vagonpanic@gmail.com.

2. Information the game processes

  • Information stored on your device: game progress, level, in-game resources (hearts and coins), and settings such as language, sound, and your privacy choices. The game stores this information locally to provide its features.
  • Google Play Games and cloud saves: if you choose to sign in and use cloud saves, Google processes information associated with your Play Games account. The game uses a player ID to link your profile and stores that ID, the save update time, progress, and in-game resources in the cloud save. The game may receive your display name from Google during sign-in; it does not send that name to Firebase Analytics.
  • Firebase Analytics: only if you consent, Google processes information about how you use the game, such as screens viewed, level starts and results, resource changes, and ad rewards. Google may also process technical app and device information and an app-instance identifier. The game does not include your name or Play Games account in its custom analytics events.
  • Advertising: if you enable ads and the service is available, Unity LevelPlay and Google AdMob may process technical device information and advertising data or identifiers needed to serve and measure ads. The game may show optional rewarded ads and interstitial ads. You can manage ad consent and privacy options in the game settings; where available, the Google UMP form may also be used.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • We use locally stored game information to provide the game and its features.
  • When you sign in to Play Games or request a cloud save, we use the relevant information to link your account and synchronize your progress.
  • We use Firebase Analytics only with your consent. You can decline without affecting gameplay.
  • We use advertising services only after you enable ads and to the extent allowed by your choices in the ad privacy form.

4. Your choices and withdrawing consent

You can change your analytics consent at any time under Settings → Privacy. Turning analytics off stops further analytics collection and resets local analytics data. It does not automatically delete information already sent to Google.

You can turn ads off in the game settings. If additional ad privacy choices are required in your region, open them using the option under Privacy. Turning ads off prevents new ad requests.

Cloud saves are used only after you sign in to Play Games; you can continue playing locally. Changes to or deletion of Google Play Games account data are also subject to Google's settings and policies.

5. Recipients and international transfers

Information is shared with providers needed for the features you enable: Google (Firebase Analytics, Google Play Games, Google AdMob, and, where applicable, Google UMP) and Unity LevelPlay. These providers may process information as independent controllers under their own privacy policies. Their services may process information outside the European Economic Area; see their policies for details and information about applicable safeguards.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy

The game does not operate its own user-account system or server for storing game progress. Information is stored on your device, in your Play Games account, or by the providers listed above, depending on which services you use.

6. Data retention

Locally stored game information remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the game. Cloud saves, analytics information, and advertising information are retained by the relevant providers according to their policies and settings. Turning analytics off stops further collection but does not by itself delete information already sent.

7. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, restriction of processing, or to object to processing. You may withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. Send requests to vagonpanic@gmail.com. Requests concerning information processed by Google or Unity as independent controllers may need to be sent directly to those companies. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority; in the Czech Republic, this is the Office for Personal Data Protection .

8. Children and changes to this policy

The game does not ask you to provide your name, email address, or other contact details. If you believe that information about a child has been handled inappropriately, contact us at the email address above.

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The current version and its effective date will be published on this page.

Please note: Google Play Games, analytics, and advertising depend on their providers and your choices. Declining analytics does not limit gameplay; turning off ads only hides features that require an ad.
